Corporate Law

We advise businesses on the legal structures, agreements, and decisions that shape their operations and growth, with practical guidance tailored to each company’s goals and obligations.

Services

  • Business formation and entity selection
  • Commercial contracts and negotiations
  • Mergers and acquisitions
  • Corporate governance and regulatory compliance
  • Shareholder agreements and ownership matters
  • Commercial disputes

What to expect

We begin by understanding your business and reviewing relevant documents, identify key risks and deadlines, then advise on options and support negotiations, documentation, or dispute resolution.

Who we help

Founders, established businesses, owners, shareholders, and organizations planning a transaction or managing a commercial issue.

Services

  • Child custody and support
  • Adoption
  • Prenuptial agreements
  • Spousal support

Criminal Defense

A criminal accusation can carry serious consequences. We explain the process, protect clients’ rights, and build a defense strategy grounded in the facts and applicable law.

Services

  • Misdemeanor and felony charges
  • DUI/DWI matters
  • White-collar crime investigations and charges
  • Bail and detention hearings
  • Appeals
  • Trial defense

What to expect

We review the allegations and time-sensitive court dates, assess available evidence, explain potential options, and prepare for hearings, negotiations, trial, or appeal when appropriate.

Who we help

People under investigation, charged with an offense, facing a bail hearing, or considering an appeal.

Real Estate

Real estate decisions involve significant commitments and detailed documents. We help clients understand transaction terms, property rights, and potential disputes before taking their next step.

Services

  • Residential and commercial transactions
  • Title and purchase agreement review
  • Landlord-tenant disputes
  • Zoning and land-use matters
  • Closings
  • Financing documentation

What to expect

Who we help

Immigration

Immigration processes can be complex and deadline-sensitive. We help individuals, families, and employers understand available pathways, prepare applications, and respond to immigration proceedings.

Services

  • Family and employment visas
  • Green card applications
  • Citizenship and naturalization
  • Asylum matters
  • Deportation defense
  • Work permits

What to expect

We discuss your goals and immigration history, review eligibility and deadlines, identify supporting records, and prepare for filings, interviews, or hearings as applicable.

Who we help

Individuals and families seeking immigration benefits or responding to proceedings, as well as employers with immigration-related needs.

Personal Injury

After an injury, understanding your rights and documenting the harm can be important. We help injured people pursue insurance claims and compensation for losses through representation on a contingency-fee basis, subject to a written agreement.

Services

  • Car accidents
  • Workplace injuries
  • Medical malpractice
  • Wrongful death claims
  • Insurance claims and negotiations

What to expect

We learn how the injury occurred, review records and insurance information, assess potential claims and deadlines, then handle communications and pursue negotiation or litigation as the matter requires.

Who we help

People injured through another party’s conduct and families pursuing a wrongful death claim.
